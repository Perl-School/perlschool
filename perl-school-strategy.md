# Perl School Publishing Strategy

## Why Perl School exists

The traditional publishers have abandoned Perl. They've largely abandoned a
lot of tech books, but it's particularly noticable for technologies like Perl.
But there is still a market for Perl books (albeit smaller) - we supply that
market.

## What we publish

We publish books about Perl. It doesn't matter how niche the subject is,
we will consider publishing a book on it. This could be a new edition of
an old Perl book (as long as the rights allow it), a book on a new Perl
technology or just something that we think the Perl community will read.

## Who we publish for

The Perl community. The people who still have Perl as a useful part of
their toolbox. And the people who still believe a good book is the best
way to absorb knowledge.

## What we offer authors

* Honest feedback on your book proposal
* Hassle-free support during the writing process (life happens - if you can't make progress on the book then we won't be on your back)
* We handle the publication process (both ebook and paperback)

## How Perl School makes money

We take a cut of the royalties that authors earn.

## What success looks like by the end of 2027

* Three more authors
* Five more published books
* 1,000 copies sold across all platforms and all formats
